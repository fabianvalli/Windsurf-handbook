Practice before you need it

Use the self-rescue strategies in the 10-Step Guide’s Safety lesson to prepare for practice with your instructor. Ask the Day Leader about recovery arrangements for your session and check the current Sailing Areas before going out.

Local landmarks from the 2009 handbook

Historical guidance; club review pending. The handbook described paddling to the third dock and carrying the equipment back. If the dock was out of reach, it identified the adjacent shore or the Marina access road as possible landings. It warned that sailors in trouble near the east-end rocks by the frontage road were hard to see. Confirm today’s usable recovery sites with the Day Leader.

Historical recovery reference

Club review pending. The handbook noted that a lowered centerboard could help with tracking while paddling. It also described a long-distance method in which the rig was detached, positioned over the back of the board with part of the boom below the board beside the fin, and secured to the universal with the extra downhaul line. Review equipment-specific recovery methods with your instructor before trying them.
