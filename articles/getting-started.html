Welcome to windsurfing at Cal Sailing Club. Use this handbook alongside your lessons and on-water practice.

Join and arrange a lesson

Start with Membership for joining information. The Windsurfing lessons page explains the club’s lesson program; use Lesson registration when you are ready to book. Registration requires you to log in.

Take part in the club

CSC runs through volunteer contributions, including teaching, maintenance, and administration. See Volunteering opportunities for ways to participate.

Find your way through the handbook

Work through Before you sail to understand the club’s procedures and prepare for a session. Then use Sailing skills alongside your lessons, and Equipment & reference when you need to check equipment names or look up a term.
