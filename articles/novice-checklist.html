Use this recap to check your preparation before going on the water. Return to any section you need to review, and ask your instructor or the Day Leader about anything that is unclear.

  1. Get ready to participate. Check your membership and lesson arrangements in Getting started at CSC.
  2. Know the club’s requirements. Review Club rules and procedures for the maintained rules, rating requirements, equipment privileges, sailing areas, and opening times. Confirm the session’s restrictions and return time with the Day Leader.
  3. Prepare for the conditions. Review Safety and asking for help, check the safety equipment you need, and know how to request assistance.
  4. Check and sign out your equipment. Follow Checking equipment out and back in, and know how to complete the return and report damage when you come ashore.
  5. Plan your launch and landing. Review Launching and landing so you know how to handle your board and rig at the dock.
  6. Be ready to get back. Use Self-rescue and getting back to review recovery instruction and identify what to practice with your instructor.

This is a preparation checklist. Use the club’s ratings page, linked from Club rules and procedures, for current test requirements.
