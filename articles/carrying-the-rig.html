Lay the sail on the ground and orient it so that the mast is perpendicular to the wind, with the clew of the sail away from the wind (A in the figure). Grab the mast and lift it over your head.

Rest the sail on your head where there is a batten (B), but do not let the whole weight of the sail rest on your head (that will put a dent in the sail). If its very windy, move the sail back and forth a bit to find the balance point in the wind. The wind will hold the sail up. When you walk, it is important to keep the sail oriented in the same angle relative to the wind.

If you turn around, the sail should stay in the same orientation in relation to the wind.
